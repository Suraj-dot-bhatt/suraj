RCC Bridge over Khageri Khola, Nepal

Final-year design project · Bachelor of Civil Engineering

For my final-year project, I worked in a six-member team to design a reinforced concrete (RCC) T-beam bridge over the Khageri Khola. The work covered the full design cycle: hydrological and hydraulic assessment of the river to fix the span and clearance, a geotechnical review of the site, and structural design of the superstructure (deck slab and T-beam girders), the bearings, and the substructure (piers, abutments, and foundations). We analyzed the structure for IRC vehicle loading and detailed the reinforcement in line with IS and NBC design codes, then prepared the engineering drawings and project report. I also took part in the site visit shown above, where we studied a comparable bridge under construction to connect our design work to real field conditions.
